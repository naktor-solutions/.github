# Naktor Solutions

**We build production AI agents and knowledge systems for enterprise and the public sector.**

We take applied AI from prototype to production: conversational agents grounded in real data, retrieval systems that answer from the source, and the tooling that keeps it all reliable. We work in regulated, confidentiality-heavy environments — so correctness, traceability, and data discipline are the default, not an afterthought.

## What we do

- **Conversational AI agents & RAG** — assistants that answer from your own documents, data, and systems, with citations back to the source.
- **Knowledge platforms** — turn scattered documents and databases into a single, queryable, always-current source of truth.
- **Document intelligence** — extraction, classification, contract and invoice processing at scale.
- **AI engineering & platforms** — evaluation, observability, and internal tooling that make AI systems dependable in production.

We've shipped these across **insurance, public administration, legal, finance, and healthcare**.
